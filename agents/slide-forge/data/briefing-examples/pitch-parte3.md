### Slide 1 — Last Call (cinematic scene)

THE OPENING SHOT. Full-screen cinematic scene — almost a film still. Apocalyptic submerged world. In the foreground, a man seen from behind or from the side, with water rising up to his neck, contained desperation on his face — he has just realized.

In the background, dominating the horizon, **the Ark** — imposing vessel, ancient and technological at the same time (dark wood + Arcane blue glow #0080ff bleeding from the cracks, neon runes on the flanks). It is moving away, about to depart. The last rope being pulled in.

Heavy sky, storm on the horizon, end-of-the-world feeling. The Ark is light in the middle of the darkness — last chance of salvation.

Text overlay, at the top, white caps with blue glow, Space Grotesk bold, 60pt:

**"ÚLTIMA CHAMADA"** — exact text, do not paraphrase.

Visual reference: Interstellar + biblical flood + premium sci-fi. Volumetric lighting, high contrast, cinematic photorealism.

Atmosphere: flood. Not "last offer" — it's "last boat before the end". Brand connection: Arca**ne** = Ark. The viewer feels the water rising in their own neck. Existential weight, definitive choice. Cinematic, not commercial.
